What a license actually covers.

The short version, so nothing is a surprise after checkout. The full text lives in the terms of service.

30 days, two devices

One purchase covers a 30-day license on up to two registered devices. Moving to a new machine needs a device reset, which support handles for you.

One trial per account

A trial runs for 1 day on 1 device and is limited to one per verified account and email. Trials can be suspended when abuse is detected.

Keys are credentials

Questions

Common questions.

The things people ask before their first purchase.

Do I need Python or Node installed?

No. Releases ship as prebuilt binaries for Windows 64-bit and Linux x86_64 — extract the archive, fill in your environment file, and run the launcher.

How does payment and activation work?

You send a transfer to top up, an admin confirms it, and then you buy the license from your balance. Approval is done by hand, so it is not instant.

Where do I find my key after buying?

Under My Licenses in the member portal. It stays listed there, so you can copy it again later instead of relying on a chat history.

How many machines can one license run on?

Two registered devices. Moving to a new machine needs a device reset, which support handles for you.

What happens when the 30 days end?

The bot validates its license online on start and periodically while it runs. An expired key stops it, and nothing renews automatically — buy another 30-day license when you want to continue.

Will it keep working after a game update?

The bot reads the game's own pages, so a change on the game side can break a module until it is updated. Fixes ship as new releases; check the release notes if something stops responding.

Can automation get my game account restricted?

It can. Automation may break a game's rules, and no tool can protect your account from that. Use it on an account whose risk you accept.
